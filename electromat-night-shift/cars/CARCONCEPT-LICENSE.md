# Lot EV hull (`ev-sedan-wide.glb`)

`ev-sedan-wide.glb` is an original in-repo sedan (CC0) for Electromat Night Shift.
It is the flare1 / haunch1 four-door notchback plus one silhouette change: the
rear fender kinks outboard over the rear wheel, then returns. Closed short
trunk deck, sloped rear glass into a painted C-pillar triangle, one full-width
red fascia bar. Not a pickup. Replaces `ev-sedan-flare.glb`. No third-party mesh.
