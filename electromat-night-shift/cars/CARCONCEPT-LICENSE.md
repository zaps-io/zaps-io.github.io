# Lot EV hull (`ev-sedan-outboard.glb`)

`ev-sedan-outboard.glb` is an original in-repo sedan (CC0) for Electromat Night Shift.
It is the haunch1 four-door notchback at the same pose and size. Only the rear-arch
lip is pushed outboard of the door waist as a curve. Closed short trunk deck,
sloped rear glass, painted C-pillar, one full-width red fascia bar. Not a bigger
box. Replaces `ev-sedan-curve.glb`. No third-party mesh.
