# Lot EV hull (`ev-sedan-curve.glb`)

`ev-sedan-curve.glb` is an original in-repo sedan (CC0) for Electromat Night Shift.
It is the haunch1 four-door notchback at the same pose and size, plus a curved
Taycan haunch: the rear quarter bows outboard over the wheel, then returns.
Closed short trunk deck, sloped rear glass, painted C-pillar triangle, one
full-width red fascia bar. Not a bigger box. Replaces `ev-sedan-wide.glb`.
No third-party mesh.
