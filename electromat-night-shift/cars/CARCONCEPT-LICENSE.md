# Lot EV hull (`ev-sedan-flare.glb`)

`ev-sedan-flare.glb` is an original in-repo sedan (CC0) for Electromat Night Shift.
It is the haunch1 four-door notchback plus one silhouette change: the rear
fender is wider over the wheel than the door. Closed short trunk deck, sloped
rear glass into a painted C-pillar triangle, one full-width red fascia bar.
Not a pickup. Replaces `ev-sedan-bulge.glb`. No third-party mesh.
